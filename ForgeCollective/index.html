Who are we?

Forge Collective is a local makers club for people who want to build and learn together.

From mini web projects to simple electronics and creative prototyping.

Whether you’re brand new or already dabbling, you’ll find a friendly space to experiment, ask questions, and leave with something you made.

What do we do?

  • Coding

    Small web projects, beginner challenges, portfolio help

  • Electronics

    Arduino basics, sensors, simple circuits

  • Design & Prototyping

    Design & Prototyping: UI mockups, 3D printing, product sketches
